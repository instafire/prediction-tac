# Prediction Tac

register: product

## Product purpose
Peer-to-peer yes/no prediction pools on TAC (TON/Telegram EVM). Users deposit TAC to a vault, stake YES or NO, winners split the losing pool. Gamified with XP, streaks, achievements, leaderboard.

## Users
1. **Telegram Mini App regulars** (primary): one thumb, dark phone UI, short sessions between chats. Need instant odds, fat YES/NO targets, vault balance always visible.
2. **Desktop power users** (secondary): multi-market scanning, command palette, admin console, deeper charts.

## Brand tone
Confident sportsbook energy meets Telegram-native craft. Sharp, fast, trustworthy. Not casino neon. Not sterile bank. Not Polymarket clone.

## Anti-references
- Polymarket blue/white card grids as the whole personality
- Kalshi exchange tables that feel like brokerage software
- Generic crypto: purple→cyan gradients, glassmorphism stacks, Space Grotesk + Inter defaults only
- AI slop: side-stripe cards, gradient text, identical icon grids

## Strategic principles
1. **Odds first.** Probability is the product. Numbers dominate every market row.
2. **Telegram thumb zone.** Primary actions live bottom-half on mobile; bottom tab bar is home base.
3. **One-handed trade.** Buy YES/NO reachable without expanding if possible.
4. **Trust without boring.** On-chain transparency as quiet chrome, not a lecture.
5. **Demo is real.** Demo wallet flows must feel as polished as mainnet.
